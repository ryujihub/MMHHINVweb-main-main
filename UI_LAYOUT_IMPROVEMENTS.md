- **Better Typography**: Improved font weights and sizes

## 📱 **Responsive Design**

### **Desktop (1024px+):**
- Full horizontal layout
- All elements in single rows
- Statistics cards on the right
- Filters and actions side by side

### **Tablet (768px - 1024px):**
- Header stacks vertically
- Statistics centered
- Filters remain horizontal
- Search and export stack

### **Mobile (640px and below):**
- Complete vertical stacking
- Full-width elements
- Compact statistics
- Smaller fonts and padding

## 🎨 **Visual Enhancements**

### **Color Scheme:**
- **Primary**: Blue (#3b82f6) for icons and accents
- **Background**: Light gray gradient (#f8fafc to #e2e8f0)
- **Cards**: White with subtle shadows
- **Text**: Dark gray (#1f2937) for headings, medium gray (#6b7280) for subtitles

### **Interactive Elements:**
- **Hover Effects**: Buttons lift slightly on hover
- **Focus States**: Blue outline for form elements
- **Transitions**: Smooth 0.2s transitions for all interactions
- **Shadows**: Layered shadows for depth

### **Typography:**
- **Main Title**: 2rem, bold, with icon
- **Subtitle**: 1rem, medium gray
- **Labels**: 0.875rem, medium weight
- **Stats**: Large numbers (1.5rem) with small labels
